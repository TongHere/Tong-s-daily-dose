Today, I was talking with my friend about a type of fish — a female fish that can produce genetic copies of itself. These cloned offspring are still able to reproduce. The reason this is possible is that the female already carries a diverse set of genetic material, and when it produces eggs, the DNA is naturally distributed into the eggs, allowing them to develop successfully.

It's not like the case of a donkey and horse creating a mule, as mules can't reproduce.
