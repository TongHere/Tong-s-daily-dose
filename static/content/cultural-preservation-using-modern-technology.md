## Cultural Revival

Last weekend, my friends invited me to an event called **Musical Belongings VII: Bach and Balafon** — lautten compagney BERLIN feat. Ba Banga Nyeck & Friends.
The program explored transcultural practices, aiming to answer two main questions: How can new sounds and hybrid forms be found that elude the label of 'world music'? And what similarities exist between different playing and composing techniques?

[Event page — Humboldt Forum](https://www.humboldtforum.org/en/programm/event-reihe/concert/musical-belongings-105207/)

---

## The Performance

Hearing a Balafon, cello, violin, and clavichord all playing together was incredible. These instruments come from completely different eras and cultures, but they blended perfectly. I did notice the Balafon took a bit of a backseat during the classical pieces, but its rich, rhythmic energy really came alive during the traditional African tracks.

To me, this is what cultural revival looks like in practice. So many traditional arts are fading away as older generations pass on and younger people move away from traditional practices. But when we bridge the gap between the past and the present, these traditions get a second life.

## Heritage Gets a Modern Update

We see this all the time when heritage gets a modern update:

**Fashion:** Indigenous designers bringing traditional patterns to modern fashion runways.
[Indigenous Designers at Australian Fashion Week — The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/fashion/gallery/2026/may/11/indigenous-designers-australian-fashion-week-in-pictures)

**Music:** Traditional indigenous music fused with modern electronic sounds.
[A Tribe Called Red / The Halluci Nation (Canada)](https://www.google.com/search?q=A+Tribe+Called+Red+%2F+The+Halluci+Nation+%28Canada%29)

**Language:** Taiwan is building AI language models to preserve the native Taiwanese (Hokkien) language.
[Taiwan Language Training Data — Digitimes](https://www.digitimes.com/news/a20250707PD226/taiwan-launch-language-training-data.html)
[Taiwan-Tongues ASR Model — Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/adi-gov-tw/Taiwan-Tongues-ASR-CE-pretrained)

**Art:** AI vision models are being used to digitally restore the ancient, weathered Dunhuang murals.
[Dunhuang Mural Dataset — Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-024-03785-0)

## 結語

傳統文化隨著時間的推移消失
社會的多樣化也會跟著一起流逝
利用科技的進展保存這些獨特且豐富的社會樣貌
也能夠在這個世界上擁有創新的元素

Every culture that disappears takes with it a unique way of seeing and understanding the world. By combining modern technology with creative collaboration, we can preserve these traditions while giving them new life for future generations.
