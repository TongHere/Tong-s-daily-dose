# The Development Process

**Date:** June 29, 2026

---

## Two Perspectives on the Development Process

### 1. Product Management Process

**Phase 1 — Validation (Proof of Concept)**

- a. Define the core problem

**Phase 2 — Scoping the MVP**

- a. What are the must-have features?
- b. Map the user journey

**Phase 3 — Building and Launching**

- a. Build the prototype
- b. Beta test with a small group
- c. Gather the feedback

---

### 2. Software Development Process

| Step | Phase | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Planning & Requirement Gathering | Turn vague business requirements into concrete technical specifications |
| 2 | Architecture & Design | Prevent technical debt by making smart design choices upfront |
| 3 | Implementation (Coding) | Write clean, modular, and maintainable code based on the design specification |
| 4 | Testing & Quality Assurance (QA) | Catch the bugs |
| 5 | Deployment & Release | Ship to production |
| 6 | Operations & Maintenance | Monitor, fix, and iterate |
