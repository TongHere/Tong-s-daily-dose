# 4th June 2026

**Date:** June 4, 2026

---

As AI models continue to improve, domain knowledge is becoming increasingly important. At the same time, curiosity and motivation remain essential traits for identifying problems worth solving and driving innovation.

Every company has many unresolved problems. Often, these problems have existed for years because they are deeply connected to multiple applications, involve significant complexity, and require substantial time and effort to address — especially when large amounts of data are involved.

With today's advanced AI capabilities and the rapid progress of increasingly intelligent models, AI can interpret data, identify patterns, and generate insights more effectively than ever before. When combined with business context and domain-specific knowledge, AI can better understand what matters most and help us develop more effective solutions.

At its core, a company's success depends on solving problems. To solve them, we first need to understand the business, its processes, and the surrounding context — just as we want AI to understand them. Once we have a strong understanding of the business, we can identify opportunities where AI can be applied to address challenges and create value.

For people with strong domain expertise who also understand how to leverage technology, this is an exciting time. There are countless longstanding problems across organizations that can now be tackled in new ways. The key question is not only whether we have the right combination of domain knowledge and technology, but also whether we have the motivation, curiosity, and willingness to take on difficult challenges and solve valuable problems.

What problem we can solve with AI? Success depends on understanding the business context, evaluating AI readiness, and fostering motivation and curiosity. These factors collectively drive valuable outcomes.
